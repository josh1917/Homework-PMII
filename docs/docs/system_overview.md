# System Overview

## Company Context

**Company Type:** Mid-sized service-oriented company operating across customer support, operations, and finance.

**Short Description:**
The company delivers digital and service-based products to a geographically distributed customer base. Daily operations rely on timely information about customer activity, operational performance, and financial outcomes.

**Management / Decision Structure Relevant to the System:**

* **Top Management:** Strategic planning, performance monitoring, policy setting.
* **Middle Management:** Tactical decisions such as resource allocation, performance optimization, and process improvements.
* **Operational Staff:** Execution-level decisions based on predefined rules and daily targets.

The system primarily supports middle and top management while providing controlled operational visibility.

## System Classification

**System Type:** **Hybrid MIS–DSS**

### Justification

#### Users

* Department managers (operations, finance, customer support)
* Team leads and supervisors
* Executive management (read-only, KPI-focused)

#### Decisions Supported

* Monitoring operational efficiency
* Identifying performance gaps
* Resource and workload allocation
* Short-term corrective actions

These are structured and semi-structured decisions, characteristic of MIS with DSS elements.

#### Outputs

* Periodic management reports (daily/weekly/monthly)
* Dashboards with KPIs (e.g., service volume, resolution time, revenue indicators)
* Exception reports highlighting deviations from targets

The system focuses on information consolidation and analysis rather than transaction processing, aligning with MIS principles while supporting decision analysis typical of DSS.
